## Cross-reference lookup path

1. Obtain the buyer's FULL OE number (prefix alone is not a match).
2. Resolve the brand and filter type from the prefix/pattern above.
3. Cross-reference the full OE number to competitor numbers (e.g. MANN, Bosch, Mahle, WIX, Fram, Sakura) in a parts catalog.
4. Match the resulting part to a Huiying SKU by dimensions (panel / thread / seal) and specification.
5. Confirm the final part with Huiying sales before ordering.

## Coverage note

Brands whose OE numbering is supplier-specific or numeric-arbitrary (e.g. Ford air filters, Chevrolet/GM, and most Chinese domestic brands such as Geely, Great Wall, BYD, Chery, Changan, Roewe) do not follow a single stable public prefix pattern; for these, always work from the buyer's full OE or cross-reference number rather than a prefix.
